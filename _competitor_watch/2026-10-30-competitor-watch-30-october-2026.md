### Drinks Wholesalers

### Matthew Clark

Success: Parent company C&C Group published interim results for the six months to 31 August 2026 on 28 October 2026, confirming that the integration of Matthew Clark and Bibendum into a single structure — Matthew Clark Bibendum (MCB) — is now complete. C&C also confirmed it has completed its acquisition of Asahi UK's wholesale operations (Nectar Imports and Asahi UK's direct distribution business, first announced 11 September 2026) into MCB, alongside a long-term UK partnership for Asahi brands; C&C expects this to make a small positive contribution to MCB's FY27 performance. Group trading was described as "in line with expectations," with branded revenue (Tennent's, Bulmers etc.) up around 2–3%, helped by good weather and World Cup-related marketing. C&C is restructuring around two units going forward: C&C Brands and MCB.

Issues: Distribution revenue — the segment MCB sits within — fell around 4% at constant exchange rates, which C&C attributes to exiting lower-margin customers and ongoing market decline. C&C describes the trading environment as volatile and says the still-to-come Christmas trading period will be important to the full year outcome.

### LWC Drinks

Success: LWC's own-label spirits brand Old J Spiced Rum unveiled its biggest brand refresh since launch on 29 October 2026, ahead of its 15th anniversary in 2027. The range has grown from a single SKU at launch in 2011 to six products today.
