### Bidfood

Success: Parent company Bidcorp published a capital markets update on 10 November 2026, covering the four months to end-October 2026 (the first trading period of its FY2027). Group trading profit rose 8.2% at constant exchange rates, with gross margin up to 25% from 24% a year earlier, helped by procurement initiatives and own-brand development. The UK was one of the stronger-performing regions, with trading profit up more than 10% at constant exchange rates on sales growth of close to 5%, with improved margins. Bidcorp said trading in early November was in line with expectations as it entered the festive trading period.

Issues: Trading conditions tightened as the period progressed, with subdued consumer spending, higher energy and fuel costs, and weaker economic growth cited as headwinds; the group expects food price inflation to feed through into selling prices over the coming months.
