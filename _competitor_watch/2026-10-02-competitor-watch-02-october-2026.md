Success: Bidfood published its food and drink trends forecast for 2027 on 1 October 2026. Rhia Harry, Bidfood's research insights manager, said consumers want "more than novelty for novelty's sake", and the trends note points to around a third of consumers being willing to pay a premium for quality.
