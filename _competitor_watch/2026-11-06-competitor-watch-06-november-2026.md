### Next plc

Success: Next published its Q3 trading statement on 5 November 2026, covering the 13 weeks to 31 October 2026. Total full-price sales rose 8.1% year on year, around £33m ahead of the company's own guidance. The outperformance was entirely international: online international sales rose 32.2%, up from 23.9% in H1 and above the 20.5% pace Next had guided to. Full-year pre-tax profit guidance was maintained at £1.255bn (up 8.4% on 2025/26's £1.158bn), following four upgrades earlier in the year, and full-year total group sales guidance was raised to 7.6% growth (to £7.54bn), up from the previous 6.9% (£7.49bn).

Issues: UK trading softened. UK full-price sales growth slowed to 2.0% in the quarter (from 3.6% in H1, though still in line with guidance), UK online sales growth slowed to 4.4% (from 7.4% in H1), and UK retail sales fell 1.7%. Next pointed to unseasonably warm September weather and called UK trading "volatile." Higher EU tariff costs also offset some of the profit upside from the strong international performance.
