### Kingfisher Group (B&Q, Screwfix, Castorama, Brico Dépôt)

Success: Kingfisher announced on 13 October 2026 that Sebastian Gundel, former CEO of German DIY retailer OBI, has been appointed as the group's new Chief Executive. He joins the Board on the same date his appointment takes effect — 1 February 2027 — bringing over two decades of leadership experience in home improvement retail, including his OBI role and an earlier spell as OBI's Chief Customer & Digital Officer. Kingfisher shares rose around 1% on the announcement.

Issues: Current CEO Thierry Garnier, who has led Kingfisher for seven years (and spent over two decades at Carrefour before that), will step down on 31 December 2026. He is leaving to become CEO of Ahold Delhaize, the Zaandam-based grocery group — Kingfisher had already flagged in May 2026 that he'd resigned to take up a senior role elsewhere. CFO Bhavesh Mistry will serve as interim CEO through January 2027 to bridge the gap before Gundel starts. Chair Claudia Arney stays on until the 2029 AGM. Separately, two routine Director/PDMR Shareholding notifications (14 and 16 October 2026) and a Transaction in Own Shares notice (13 October 2026, part of the ongoing buyback) were filed — no trading implications.
