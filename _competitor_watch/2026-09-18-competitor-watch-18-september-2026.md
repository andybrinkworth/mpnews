### Next plc

Success: Next published half-year results on 17 September 2026 (H1 to 1 August 2026). Pre-tax profit rose 10.5% to £569m, full-price sales grew 7.7% in H1, and the company raised full-year profit guidance for the fourth time this year — up £12m to £1.255bn. International online sales continued to grow strongly (over 20% year-on-year). Shares rose around 3% on the announcement.

Issues: Next flagged a slowdown in UK consumer spending for the second half, citing inflation, higher mortgage costs and a weak labour market. H2 UK sales growth guidance was trimmed to 2.0% (from 2.8%), and overall full-price sales growth is expected to decelerate to 5.8% in H2, down from 7.7% in H1.
