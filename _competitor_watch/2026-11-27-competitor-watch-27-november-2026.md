### Kingfisher Group (B&Q, Screwfix, Castorama, Brico Dépôt)

Success: Kingfisher published its Q3 26/27 trading update on 24 November 2026, covering the quarter to 31 October 2026, and upgraded full-year profit guidance to £610m–£640m adjusted pre-tax profit. Total sales including marketplace GMS rose 2.4%, with underlying like-for-like sales up 0.8%, driven by higher customer transactions. Performance was led by Screwfix (UK & Ireland LFL +6.9%) and strong growth in Poland and Iberia (LFL +6.6%), with market share gains at B&Q, Screwfix, Castorama France, Poland and Spain.

Issues: B&Q's UK like-for-like sales fell 1.0% in the quarter (core +0.3%, big-ticket -6.3%, seasonal -1.1%), and Brico Dépôt France fell 5.3% on weaker demand in building materials and larger project categories.
