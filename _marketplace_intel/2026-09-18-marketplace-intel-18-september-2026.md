*Covering 15–18 September 2026, since the last digest.*

### Top 3

- **FTC expanded the Amazon Prime settlement redress programme (17 Sept).** A court-approved revised order raises the per-consumer refund cap from $51 to $200, widens eligibility to consumers who used 11–20 Prime benefits in a year (previously excluded), and moves to automatic payment with no claims process needed; Amazon has paid out over $845m of the original $1.5bn requirement so far. *Why it matters: layered on top of the 2 September ad-auction pricing suit, this keeps consumer-protection and antitrust cost pressure on Amazon compounding heading into peak trading — a reminder of the compliance overhead that comes with dominant-marketplace scale.*

- **eBay decoupled livestream sales from core seller-performance scoring (15 Sept), then extended two features (16 Sept): Fitment Plus Auto to the UK, and Authenticity Guarantee to watches in Australia.** Live sales no longer count toward Top Rated Plus, Seller Standards or service metrics. *Why it matters: a concrete example of a marketplace re-engineering seller-scoring rules as it adds new selling formats — any commission marketplace that diversifies beyond one listing type eventually has to decide whether to blend or ring-fence performance metrics across formats.*

- **Amazon confirmed UK Prime Big Deal Days for 6–7 October (announced 15 Sept), while Retail Dive reported Amazon and Target are both readying separate October sales events (15 Sept).** *Why it matters: the holiday promotional calendar keeps creeping earlier into Q4, compressing sellers' pricing and stock-planning windows ahead of the FBA peak-fee and surcharge stack already flagged in the background section above.*

### Wider roundup

- Lowe's added Lowes.com and its third-party Marketplace to CMO Jen Wilson's remit alongside retail media and personalisation (Retail Dive, 16 Sept) — another data point for marketplace ownership consolidating under commercial/digital leadership rather than sitting as a standalone unit.

- Amazon Accelerate 2026, Amazon's annual seller conference, is confirmed for 22–24 September in Seattle, with speakers being announced this week (16 Sept). It falls just after this digest's window — worth watching the next entry for any holiday-readiness or fee/tooling announcements that come out of it.

- No new dated developments surfaced this week in the FTC v Amazon ad-pricing suit, the EU DMA gatekeeper case, or Shein/Temu litigation beyond what's already recorded in the background section — those stories are static for now, not new.

### B2B relevance for Booker Marketplace

- Genuinely new, dated UK wholesale/B2B marketplace competitor news was thin this week — nothing dated from Bestway, Nisa, Musgrave, Costco UK or Amazon Business UK turned up in the 15–18 September window, so this section is short by design rather than padded.

- The one transferable point is eBay's move to ring-fence a new selling format (livestream) from its core seller-standards scoring: as Booker Marketplace adds features, categories or supplier cohorts, keeping performance metrics format-aware — rather than blending everything into a single score — avoids penalising genuine experimentation by suppliers trying something new.

- Lowe's folding its marketplace into a combined retail-media/digital-commerce remit is a small but relevant governance signal for any B2B platform still settling where marketplace sits organisationally relative to commercial and digital teams.
