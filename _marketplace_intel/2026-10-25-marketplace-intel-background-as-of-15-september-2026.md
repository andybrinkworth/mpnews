### Last 7 days — 8–15 September 2026

- **FTC and 22 state AGs sued Amazon over ad-auction pricing (2 Sept).** Alleges Amazon inflated advertising auction prices after 2019, extracting over $20bn from advertisers. Why it matters: another front in the antitrust pressure on dominant marketplaces — advertising economics, not just seller fees or Buy Box rules, are now a legal battleground.

- **Amazon tightened insurance requirements for "enhanced safety" categories (7 Sept, effective 2 Nov).** Sellers in these categories now need $1m commercial liability cover regardless of sales volume, replacing the old $10k/month threshold; mainland China sellers must use Amazon's own Insurance Accelerator. Why it matters: Amazon is raising the compliance bar (and steering sellers toward its own insurance product) — a model other marketplaces tend to follow once Amazon sets the precedent.

- **Amazon stacked peak-season surcharges.** Shipping peak surcharges run 25 Oct–16 Jan, layering on top of FBA peak fees (from 15 Oct) and an existing 3.5% fuel/logistics surcharge. Why it matters: seller-side cost inflation ahead of Black Friday/Christmas — squeezes margin for anyone selling through Amazon's fulfilment network into peak.

- **Amazon Pan-EU FBA now requires an active Netherlands offer (from 3 Sept; Belgium follows Feb 2027).** Why it matters: Amazon is quietly forcing full EU marketplace coverage as the price of Pan-EU fulfilment — a lever other marketplaces could copy to force geographic completeness.

- **France's anti-fast-fashion levy took effect (1 Sept).** Per-item fees on ultra-fast-fashion, rising toward ~€20 by 2030. Why it matters: first live example of a national government taxing a marketplace business model directly (Shein/Temu-style) rather than just goods — a template other EU states may copy.

- **TikTok Shop reshuffled Seller Center navigation (7 Sept) and tightened packaging/shipping rules (3 Sept).** Why it matters: routine but signals TikTok Shop is maturing its seller operations toward Amazon-grade rigour as it scales.

- **Amazon expanded Price History from 90 to 365 days.** Gives shoppers a full year of price context. Why it matters: response to "fake discount" scrutiny — pricing transparency is becoming table stakes, not a differentiator.

- **Google's August 2026 spam update finished rolling out (21 Aug)**, and Google Shopping is ramping AI placements for Q4 — sellers advised to add Q&A content, spec sheets and variant-popularity signals. Why it matters: product-data quality is now an AI-discoverability requirement, not just an SEO nicety — directly relevant to any marketplace's product-data strategy.

- **eMarketer's holiday forecast: 2.6% overall retail growth, 6.6% e-commerce growth for the 2026 season.** Why it matters: e-commerce (and marketplace) growth is again outpacing total retail by a wide margin — the channel shift keeps compounding.

### Last month — mid-August to mid-September 2026

(condensed to what's still being talked about, not a day-by-day log)

- **Walmart's "Let's Grow!" Marketplace Seller Summit (late Aug):** targeted fee reductions, an AI seller assistant, expanded next-day delivery and physical-store presence; marketplace sales up 17% YoY and past 200,000 active sellers. Why it matters: Walmart is now explicitly competing with Amazon on seller economics, not just consumer price — fee reductions from the #2 marketplace put pressure on everyone's take-rate.

- **Shopify rolled out WebMCP to all Liquid storefronts (5 Aug)** and followed with tools letting merchants track sales/traffic from AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot), plus an AI "Campaign Autopilot" for ads. Why it matters: Shopify is building the infrastructure layer for AI agents to browse, query and buy directly from stores — the first mainstream move from "AI as marketing channel" to "AI as sales channel."

- **OpenAI/Stripe's Agentic Commerce Protocol and ChatGPT Instant Checkout continued rolling out to more merchants (Shopify, Etsy integrations).** Why it matters: a real, named checkout protocol for AI agents now exists and is live — the plumbing for "agentic commerce" is no longer theoretical.

- **Sur La Table launched "Second Servings" (25 Aug),** a peer-to-peer recommerce marketplace for pre-owned kitchen goods, with third-party logistics support. Why it matters: another established retailer bolting on a resale marketplace as a retention/sustainability play — a pattern spreading well beyond fashion.

- **Amazon's FTC/state ad-pricing lawsuit (2 Sept) is still the dominant antitrust story a month on**, alongside continued coverage of the stacked peak-season fee increases. Why it matters: both are shaping seller sentiment heading into the busiest trading quarter of the year.

- **France's fast-fashion levy (1 Sept) is being watched as a precedent** by other EU regulators. Why it matters: could presage similar levies elsewhere in Europe aimed at low-cost cross-border marketplace sellers.

- **F&F Ventures launched a $10m e-commerce fund with SHOPLINE**, backing pre-seed–Series A consumer brands with platform infrastructure access. Why it matters: platform vendors are increasingly investing directly in the brands that sell on them — blurring the line between infrastructure provider and equity investor.

### Last 12 months — September 2025 to September 2026

(pivotal moments only — what you'd need to know to not be caught out in conversation)

1. **The US ended the global de minimis exemption (effective 29 Aug 2025), and its effects have dominated cross-border marketplace economics through 2026.** Temu, Shein and AliExpress have had to restructure pricing, fulfilment and seller models around duties that previously didn't apply. Why it matters: this is the single biggest structural shock to low-cost marketplace models in years — it changed the economics of ultra-cheap cross-border selling globally, not just in the US.

1. **Mirakl reported full-year profitability for FY2025** (ARR $218m, +23%; GMV ~$15bn, +31%), with its Mirakl Connect and Mirakl Ads products both scaling fast, and ~20% of R&D now going to AI/agentic tooling. Why it matters: direct read on the health of the commission-marketplace software category — the platform Booker Marketplace is built on is growing and investing heavily in AI, not standing still.

1. **Agentic commerce arrived as infrastructure, not hype** — OpenAI/Stripe's Agentic Commerce Protocol, ChatGPT Instant Checkout, Shopify's WebMCP, and Google's push into AI shopping placements all launched or scaled within the year. Why it matters: a new discovery-and-checkout layer is forming above traditional marketplaces — every platform now needs an answer for "how does an AI agent buy from us."

1. **EU General Court partially annulled a Digital Markets Act gatekeeper designation (Meta/Facebook Marketplace, June 2026)**, ruling the Commission used an incorrect timeframe and insufficient reasoning. Why it matters: first real judicial pushback on DMA marketplace-gatekeeper enforcement — signals the regulatory net around large marketplaces isn't tightening in a straight line.

1. **Walmart Marketplace crossed 200,000 active sellers with sales up 17% YoY**, while rolling out fee cuts and AI seller tools. Why it matters: Walmart is now a credible #2 commission marketplace at real scale, not just an Amazon alternative in name.

1. **UK retailers kept proving the "retailer becomes marketplace/platform" model**: B&Q's marketplace reached ~45% of its e-commerce sales and 2,000+ third-party sellers, adding marketplace click & collect as a "UK first"; Next's Total Platform keeps extending Next's infrastructure to other retailers as a service. Why it matters: closest domestic precedent for Booker's own commission-model build — proof that UK retailers can scale third-party marketplace revenue to a large share of digital sales.

1. **Faire's very public "we grew too fast and nearly broke the company" reset became a cautionary case study for B2B wholesale marketplaces.** Why it matters: directly relevant to Booker's dropship/marketplace build — a reminder that wholesale marketplace growth without operational discipline (fulfilment, credit, supplier onboarding) can outrun the business.

1. **TikTok Shop pushed aggressive European expansion, targeting roughly 200 million EU users in 2026.** Why it matters: social commerce is no longer a US/China phenomenon — it's now a genuine third marketplace channel in Europe alongside Amazon and native retailer platforms.

1. **Amazon faced a second major antitrust front (FTC + 22 states, Sept 2026) on top of existing scrutiny**, while simultaneously tightening seller insurance requirements and stacking peak-season fees. Why it matters: regulatory and cost pressure on the dominant marketplace are rising together — a climate that tends to push sellers toward diversifying across marketplaces.

1. **National-level taxation of fast-fashion/low-cost marketplace models began in earnest (France's per-item levy, Sept 2026), following the broader de minimis shake-up.** Why it matters: governments are moving from trade-rule tweaks to direct fiscal targeting of marketplace business models — a trend worth watching for any commission-based platform.
