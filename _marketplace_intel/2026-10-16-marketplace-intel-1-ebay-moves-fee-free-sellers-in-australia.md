title: "Marketplace Intel — 1. eBay moves fee-free sellers in Australia into managed shipping, following its UK and US playbook (announced 9 Oct, phased rollout from 11 Nov)"
date: 2026-10-16
tags: ["marketplace", "ai"]
link: ""
---

Under a User Agreement update, eBay will calculate shipping cost from the buyer's location and parcel details, collect postage directly from buyers, and issue prepaid Australia Post labels for eligible domestic sales by sellers without a Pro plan; sellers offering "free shipping" instead pay a discounted flat rate out of their proceeds. Sellers lose control over carrier choice and can't cancel or replace a prepaid label without risking delivery protections; keeping full control over shipping now requires upgrading to a Pro plan.

Why it matters: this is the third market in a year - after the UK's Simple Delivery mandate and the US "Ship With eBay" expansion, both covered in the last two digests - where eBay has traded seller choice over shipping for its own cut of the logistics flow. UK sellers reportedly saw rates rise once moved onto managed shipping, so Australian sellers may see the same.
