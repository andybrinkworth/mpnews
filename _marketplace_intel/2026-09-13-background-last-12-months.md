*Compiled as of 15 September 2026 — pivotal moments only, not a comprehensive log.*

1. **The US ended the global de minimis exemption (effective 29 Aug 2025), and its effects have dominated cross-border marketplace economics through 2026.** Temu, Shein and AliExpress have had to restructure pricing, fulfilment and seller models around duties that previously didn't apply. *Why it matters: this is the single biggest structural shock to low-cost marketplace models in years — it changed the economics of ultra-cheap cross-border selling globally, not just in the US.*

1. **Mirakl reported full-year profitability for FY2025** (ARR $218m, +23%; GMV ~$15bn, +31%), with its Mirakl Connect and Mirakl Ads products both scaling fast, and ~20% of R&D now going to AI/agentic tooling. *Why it matters: direct read on the health of the commission-marketplace software category — the platform Booker Marketplace is built on is growing and investing heavily in AI, not standing still.*

1. **Agentic commerce arrived as infrastructure, not hype** — OpenAI/Stripe's Agentic Commerce Protocol, ChatGPT Instant Checkout, Shopify's WebMCP, and Google's push into AI shopping placements all launched or scaled within the year. *Why it matters: a new discovery-and-checkout layer is forming above traditional marketplaces — every platform now needs an answer for "how does an AI agent buy from us."*

1. **EU General Court partially annulled a Digital Markets Act gatekeeper designation (Meta/Facebook Marketplace, June 2026)**, ruling the Commission used an incorrect timeframe and insufficient reasoning. *Why it matters: first real judicial pushback on DMA marketplace-gatekeeper enforcement — signals the regulatory net around large marketplaces isn't tightening in a straight line.*

1. **Walmart Marketplace crossed 200,000 active sellers with sales up 17% YoY**, while rolling out fee cuts and AI seller tools. *Why it matters: Walmart is now a credible #2 commission marketplace at real scale, not just an Amazon alternative in name.*

1. **UK retailers kept proving the "retailer becomes marketplace/platform" model**: B&Q's marketplace reached ~45% of its e-commerce sales and 2,000+ third-party sellers, adding marketplace click & collect as a "UK first"; Next's Total Platform keeps extending Next's infrastructure to other retailers as a service. *Why it matters: closest domestic precedent for Booker's own commission-model build — proof that UK retailers can scale third-party marketplace revenue to a large share of digital sales.*

1. **Faire's very public "we grew too fast and nearly broke the company" reset became a cautionary case study for B2B wholesale marketplaces.** *Why it matters: directly relevant to Booker's dropship/marketplace build — a reminder that wholesale marketplace growth without operational discipline (fulfilment, credit, supplier onboarding) can outrun the business.*

1. **TikTok Shop pushed aggressive European expansion, targeting roughly 200 million EU users in 2026.** *Why it matters: social commerce is no longer a US/China phenomenon — it's now a genuine third marketplace channel in Europe alongside Amazon and native retailer platforms.*

1. **Amazon faced a second major antitrust front (FTC + 22 states, Sept 2026) on top of existing scrutiny**, while simultaneously tightening seller insurance requirements and stacking peak-season fees. *Why it matters: regulatory and cost pressure on the dominant marketplace are rising together — a climate that tends to push sellers toward diversifying across marketplaces.*

1. **National-level taxation of fast-fashion/low-cost marketplace models began in earnest (France's per-item levy, Sept 2026), following the broader de minimis shake-up.** *Why it matters: governments are moving from trade-rule tweaks to direct fiscal targeting of marketplace business models — a trend worth watching for any commission-based platform.*
