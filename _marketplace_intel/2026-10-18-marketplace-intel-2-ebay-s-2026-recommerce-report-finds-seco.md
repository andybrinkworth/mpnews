Surveying over 10,500 consumers across 10 countries, eBay found 76% bought secondhand in the past year, with 47% buying at least monthly - up from 35% in 2025; 70% see secondhand shopping as important for managing household budgets (36% of US respondents call it "very important"); and 89% plan to spend the same or more on secondhand next year, rising to 53% of 18-29-year-olds expecting to buy more. On the seller side, 45% of surveyed US eBay sellers say resale provides supplemental income and 25% say it's vital household income.

Why it matters: hard survey data, not anecdote, behind a trend this digest has tracked all year (Vinted/Depop's shipping price war, Sur La Table's resale launch, J.C. Penney's marketplace) - recommerce is now a budget-management habit for a large share of shoppers, not a sustainability niche.
