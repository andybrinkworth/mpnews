- The NRF's 2026 holiday forecast (15 Oct) projects US November–December retail sales of $1.013 trillion, up 4.2% on 2025 — a faster pace than 2025's holiday growth. It doesn't break out an online/e-commerce figure, so it sits alongside rather than replaces Adobe's online-specific forecast already on record (6.7% online growth, Cyber Monday topping $15bn).

- TikTok Shop Malaysia's commission-rate rise across most categories, flagged in the last digest for 31 October, remains on track; the specific old and new rates still haven't appeared in available reporting.

- The next genuinely busy stretch for marketplace news is imminent rather than arrived: eBay and Etsy report Q3 2026 results on 28 October, Amazon on 29 October, and Amazon Business's "Reshape 2026" B2B procurement conference runs 27–28 October in Nashville — all likely sources of substantial new data for the next digest.

- No new dated developments surfaced this window in the FTC v Amazon ad-auction pricing suit, the FTC's separate Amazon monopolisation case (still on its 29 March 2027 trial date), the EU DMA Meta/Facebook Marketplace gatekeeper case, the EU Commission's Amazon price-parity probe, France's fast-fashion levy, the EU's confirmed 1 November Union Handling Fee, or the EU's mandatory customs Product Identifier rule — those all remain as previously recorded. Texas's proposed repeal of its marketplace-fee tax (Rule 3.330) also remains in its public-comment period (through 15 November), with no update this window.
