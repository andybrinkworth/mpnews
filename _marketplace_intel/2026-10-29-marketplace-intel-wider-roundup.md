- The EU has now confirmed the exact size of the per-item "Union Handling Fee" flagged as still-to-be-set in the last digest: €2 per item, on top of the existing €3 duty, from 1 November 2026 (confirmed via an Irish Revenue notice, 29 Sept) — paid by whichever business files the customs declaration, regardless of the parcel's value.

- Shopify launched "Canvas" (1 Oct), which lets merchants build a storefront by chatting with its Sidekick AI rather than editing a theme directly, showing real-time rendered changes. It's desktop-only for now and doesn't yet support third-party themes, app blocks or extensions — it joins similar AI site-builders from Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and Framer.

- eBay has raised seller currency-conversion fees by 0.25 percentage points in every market it operates — US/Canada (3% to 3.25%, from 14 Oct), Australia (to 3.25%, 1 Nov), UK (2.5% to 2.75%, 1 Dec) and Germany/France/Italy/Spain (to 3.25%, 2 Dec) — without a formal Seller Update or Community post; the change was only added to each market's selling-fee page (reported 3 Oct).

- Amazon's UK Prime Big Deal Days ran 6–7 October, as flagged in the last digest; no performance figures are out yet.

- No new dated developments surfaced this window in the FTC v Amazon ad-auction pricing suit, the EU DMA Meta/Facebook Marketplace gatekeeper case, the EU Commission's Amazon price-parity probe, or France's fast-fashion levy — those remain as previously recorded. Separately, the long-running FTC monopoly/marketplace case against Amazon (distinct from the ad-auction suit) — once scheduled for an October 2026 trial — has since slipped to a proposed 29 March 2027, so nothing in that case is imminent despite older coverage still circulating with an October 2026 date.
