- **Etsy's September seller update (22 Sept)** added Shared Shop Access (up to five collaborators per shop, with permission limits — no access to finances or refunds), a third listing variation and more customisable option fields, a redesigned payment/balance view, and wider eligibility for its listing-appeals process (aiming for most sellers to have access across most policy areas by end of 2026). Etsy also launched "Holiday HQ" (24 Sept), a hub to help sellers prepare for peak season.

- No new dated developments surfaced this window in the EU Commission's price-parity probe into Amazon (flagged 18–21 Sept, still preliminary), the FTC v Amazon ad-auction pricing suit, the EU DMA Meta/Facebook Marketplace gatekeeper case, or Shein/Temu litigation — those remain as previously recorded.
