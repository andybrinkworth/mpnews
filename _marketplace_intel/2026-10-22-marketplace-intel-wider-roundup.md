- TikTok Shop confirmed its Black Friday/Cyber Monday campaign window will run 11–30 November (announced 19 Oct) — another sign of how far in advance the platform now locks in its peak-season promotional calendar.

- TikTok Shop Malaysia will raise commission fees across most categories from 31 October (reported 19 Oct); the specific old and new rates weren't given in available reporting.

- Nothing lands in the earnings calendar this specific window, but the next one will be busy: eBay and Etsy both report Q3 2026 results on 28 October, and Amazon on 29 October — all worth watching in the next digest for seller-fee, GMV and agentic-commerce commentary.

- Amazon Business's "Reshape 2026" B2B procurement conference (27–28 October, Nashville) remains the most likely near-term source of further B2B marketplace news; nothing new has come out of it yet beyond the already-published agenda.

- No new dated developments surfaced this window in the FTC v Amazon ad-auction pricing suit, the FTC's separate Amazon monopolisation case (still on its 29 March 2027 trial date), the EU DMA Meta/Facebook Marketplace gatekeeper case, the EU Commission's Amazon price-parity probe, France's fast-fashion levy, the EU's confirmed 1 November Union Handling Fee, or the EU's mandatory customs Product Identifier rule — those all remain as previously recorded. Texas's proposed repeal of its marketplace-fee tax (Rule 3.330) also remains in its public-comment period (through 15 November), with no update this window.
