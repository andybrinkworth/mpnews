- Genuinely new, dated UK wholesale/B2B marketplace competitor news was thin yet again this window — nothing dated from Bestway, Nisa, Musgrave, Costco UK or Amazon Business UK turned up in the 6–9 October window, so this stays short by design rather than padded.

- The FCC's new liability split for US marketplaces — platform liability scaling with how much physical control or title the platform has over the goods — is the most transferable point this week. It's an early, concrete example of a regulator formalising exactly the kind of platform-vs-supplier compliance question (product safety, recalls, labelling) that Booker Marketplace will need its own clear policy on as its third-party supplier base grows, regardless of jurisdiction.

- eBay's category-specific seller perks (certification status, a lower authentication threshold, bulk tooling, a dedicated fitment service) are a reminder that seller/supplier loyalty can be built through recognition and tooling within a category, not only through commission or fee concessions — a cheaper lever worth keeping in mind for Booker's own supplier engagement as the platform matures.

- Amazon Business's Reshape conference (27–28 October) is worth flagging now so it isn't missed — it's the most likely near-term source of genuine B2B marketplace news given how quiet this window has been.
