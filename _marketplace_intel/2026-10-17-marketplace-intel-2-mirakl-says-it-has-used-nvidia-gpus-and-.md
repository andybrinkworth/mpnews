The claim appeared in an NVIDIA roundup of French companies building on its hardware, published nine days ahead of Jensen Huang's GTC Berlin keynote. NVIDIA says Mirakl has onboarded over 70 million seller products using fine-tuned and open models running on its GPUs and inference software, cutting catalogue onboarding time to "under two hours." No baseline onboarding time, processing period or model names were given, so the scale of the improvement can't be independently checked - every figure in the post comes from NVIDIA or its customers. Mirakl's platform underpins more than 450 marketplaces and 100,000+ third-party sellers; faster, cleaner product-data ingestion also feeds Mirakl Ads, its advertising business, since sponsored placement needs structured catalogue data first.

Why it matters: this is the platform Booker Marketplace itself is built on, making it the most directly relevant item this digest has carried in weeks rather than an analogy. If faster, AI-assisted catalogue onboarding reaches Booker's own Mirakl instance, it could shorten the time between a new supplier joining and their full range being live and sponsorable - worth a direct question to the Mirakl account team on timing and availability, independent of NVIDIA's own figures.
