UPS subsidiary Happy Returns will handle TikTok Shop returns at nearly 10,000 drop-off points (including UPS stores, Staples and Ulta Beauty), letting buyers hand back eligible items without printing a label or finding a box; staff scan and consolidate items for shipment, with AI-based fraud screening before refunds are issued. UPS cites returns running at roughly 27% of purchase price and almost 20% of online orders overall, against a ~$850bn US retail returns bill in 2025.

Why it matters: a concrete example of a social-commerce marketplace outsourcing an entire operational headache - reverse logistics - to a carrier's existing network rather than building it in-house, at a moment when returns volume and fraud risk are both rising industry-wide.
