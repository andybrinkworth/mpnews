date: 2026-10-17
tags: ["marketplace", "ai"]
link: ""

At its "Dash Forward" event in San Francisco, DoorDash said Macy's (350+ stores), Anthropologie (220+ stores) and VF Corporation brands including The North Face, Vans and Timberland (350+ stores) are now live on its marketplace, alongside a new partnership with Costco that brings eight of the top ten US grocers onto the platform. It also launched "Dasher Returns": a courier can collect an eligible return in as little as 30 minutes for a flat $7.99 fee, carrying it back to the originating store for restocking and a refund within days; the service is live in select cities now, with nationwide expansion due in November. DoorDash said over half of the National Retail Federation's Top 100 Retailers now sell through it, and roughly 90% of a retailer's first-time DoorDash shoppers are incremental customers.

Why it matters: a delivery platform is explicitly repositioning its existing driver and store network as general-purpose retail and reverse-logistics infrastructure - its chief revenue officer described it as turning "the stores they already have into a growth channel, not a warehouse three states away" - a live example of marketplace-as-a-service and outsourced returns handling arriving in the same announcement.
