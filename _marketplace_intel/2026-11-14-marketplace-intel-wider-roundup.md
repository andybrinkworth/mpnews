- Shopify reports Q3 2026 results before markets open today (3 November, 8:30am ET); figures weren't available as this digest was being compiled, so they're flagged for the next entry rather than estimated here.

- TikTok Shop's Black Friday/Cyber Monday campaign (announced 21 October, just outside this window but now imminent) runs 11–30 November, leaning on livestreams from major brands — Sephora, Michael Kors, Best Buy, Ulta Beauty and Victoria's Secret are named partners. TikTok says US live-shopping sales more than doubled in the first half of 2026 versus a year earlier, and a single Fenty Beauty livestream in October drew over a million viewers; last year's four-day BFCM weekend topped $500m in sales on the platform.

- Meta's contested AI-training/website-crawling Commercial Terms remain unresolved, with no material change since the last two digests: Meta told Reuters on 23 October the effective date has moved from 30 October to 20 November, but as of 24 October its own published terms pages still showed the original 30 October date, and the underlying AI-training and crawling clauses are unchanged. UK publisher groups continue to call the delay "a pause, not a solution."

- No new dated developments surfaced this window in the FTC v Amazon ad-auction pricing suit, the FTC's separate Amazon monopolisation case (still on its 29 March 2027 trial date, after Amazon's bid to push it to June 2027 was rejected), the EU DMA Meta/Facebook Marketplace gatekeeper case, the EU Commission's Amazon price-parity probe, or France's fast-fashion levy — those all remain as previously recorded. Texas's proposed repeal of its marketplace-fee tax (Rule 3.330) also remains in its public-comment period, through 15 November.

- Genuinely new, dated UK wholesale/B2B marketplace competitor news was thin again this window — nothing dated from Bestway, Nisa, Musgrave, Costco UK or Amazon Business UK turned up in the 31 October–3 November window, so this stays short by design rather than padded.
