Despite projections such as Mastercard's $1 trillion by 2030 for agentic US retail, AI strategist Lindsay Walker (Hedera AI Studio) was quoted saying "agentic commerce is not a reality yet" and estimating five years before it is genuinely workable. Cited barriers: today's probabilistic AI models aren't reliable enough for deterministic financial transactions, most merchant sites still lack the account-access and data-retrieval infrastructure an autonomous agent needs, and there's no mature way to tether an agent's identity to the human it's acting for. Amazon's blocking of Meta's Muse and Perplexity's shopping agents was cited as evidence platforms themselves remain unconvinced.

Why it matters: a useful counterweight to the steady run of agentic-commerce launches already tracked in this digest (Instacart's Clementine, Meta's Muse, Shopify's WebMCP, Amazon's Claude plugin) - credible outside voices are now saying the infrastructure and trust layer is still years from solid, which matters for how aggressively any platform should move now versus later.
