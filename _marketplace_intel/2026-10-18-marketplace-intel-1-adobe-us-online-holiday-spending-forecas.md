Adobe's holiday forecast (1 Nov-31 Dec) projects Cyber Week growing 7.4% to $47.5bn, with Cyber Monday alone passing $15bn online for the first time (+6.2%) and Black Friday up 9.2% to $12.9bn on electronics and appliances discounting. Adobe also flags a stockpiling trend layered on top of gift-buying: online sales of essentials are set to jump sharply during Cyber Week (personal care +150%, clothing basics +210%, baby products +113%, pet items +93%).

Why it matters: a more bullish, more granular read than the eMarketer holiday forecast already on record (2.6% retail / 6.6% e-commerce growth) - the essentials-stockpiling pattern in particular suggests shoppers are treating online more like a general restocking channel, not just a gift channel, which is worth factoring into Q4 category and delivery-capacity planning.
