Booker

Tesco's interim results, published 8 October 2026, included Booker's first-half performance. Reporting varied slightly by outlet: Retail Insight Network (9 October 2026) reported Booker's adjusted operating profit at £163m, with like-for-like sales down 2.6%. BritBrief (8 October 2026) reported operating profit as "slightly up" — helped by better buying terms and Tesco's "Save to Invest" cost-efficiency programme offsetting lower sales volumes — with sales excluding tobacco down 1.4%, tobacco sales down 8.9% to £740m (blamed on regulation, tax rises and shifting habits), and two-year growth excluding tobacco up 2.7%. KamCity's 9 October 2026 headline on the same results was "Tesco Sees Booker Making Progress Despite Weak Half Year Figures" (full text behind a paywall). The precise percentages differ between outlets, likely reflecting different reporting bases (e.g. total like-for-like versus ex-tobacco sales); nothing has been reconciled or guessed at beyond what each source states.

Tesco

Tesco published its interim results for the 26 weeks to 29 August 2026 on 8 October 2026. Group adjusted operating profit rose 6.5% to £1.78bn, and statutory operating profit rose 6.6% to £1.70bn. Group revenue (including fuel) grew 3.7% to £37.35bn, with group like-for-like sales up 1%. Tesco raised its full-year 2026/27 group adjusted operating profit guidance to £3.15bn–£3.30bn, up from the £3.0bn–£3.3bn range set in April. The share buyback programme was increased to £950m from £750m, and capital expenditure guidance rose to around £1.7bn from around £1.6bn. Chief executive Ken Murphy described consumer confidence as "relatively resilient," while flagging geopolitical uncertainty. Tesco's next scheduled update is its third-quarter and Christmas trading statement, due 14 January 2027. (Booker-specific figures from the same results are covered in the Booker section above.)
