Booker Wholesale

Tesco and Booker have installed more than 500 digital advertising screens across 100 Booker wholesale locations, turning the cash & carry network into a retail media channel that brands can pay to use. The screens are 65-inch Sony BZ35L BRAVIA Professional Displays, mounted mainly at rack ends and above palletised stock so adverts sit next to the products they promote. The rollout took eight weeks and was scheduled outside peak trading hours to avoid disrupting trading.

Three companies worked on the project alongside Booker and Tesco: dunnhumby, Tesco's retail media arm, led on the commercial side; Pixel Inspiration, a managed digital media provider, installed the network and provides ongoing 24/7 support and onsite maintenance; and Sony supplied the displays. Sony's displays use a built-in Android system on chip, so the installation did not need separate external media players, and Sony's Remote Device Manager lets the network be monitored and updated without regular engineer visits. Pixel Inspiration worked with mount manufacturer UNICOL on protective mounting designed to withstand a busy cash & carry environment. Campaigns are managed centrally through the DISE content platform rather than by individual depots. Karl Ingham, key account manager at Sony Professional Displays and Solutions Europe, commented on the project for the trade press, describing it as showing how digital signage can reach shoppers in "highly relevant environments."

The earliest coverage found (RetailTimes) is timestamped 19 October 2026 at 11:08 BST, after last week's edition of this digest was compiled; Grocery Gazette and Retail Gazette carried the story on 20 October 2026.

Tesco

The retail media screen rollout at Booker's depots, covered in full under Booker Wholesale above, sits within a wider push by Tesco to expand retail media across the group. According to the Grocery Gazette and Retail Gazette coverage of the launch, Tesco's half-year results show more than 7,500 digital advertising screens across the group as a whole, including those at Booker and at the convenience chain One Stop. The same results showed Tesco Media reporting a 17% increase in active advertisers and more campaigns per advertiser. The coverage also notes that Tesco is introducing influencer-led advertising across its stores, website and app, and that it has partnered with ad tech firm Kevel on a self-service advertising platform covering campaign planning, booking and measurement. Separately, the coverage refers back to a retail media alliance dunnhumby announced with Tesco, B&Q, John Lewis and Waitrose in June 2026, intended to let advertisers book campaigns across several retailers at once.

No other news touching Tesco's wholesale or Booker-related activity was found for this period. Tesco's next scheduled update is its third-quarter and Christmas trading statement, due 14 January 2027.
