### Booker

Booker held its seventh annual Foodservice Trade Show at the NEC, Birmingham, on 23 September 2026 (Hall 2, 9am–4pm) — within this digest's research window. It was announced in the trade press in advance (Cash & Carry Management, 21 August 2026) as bringing together Booker's foodservice customers, suppliers and partners. New features this year included a "Butcher's Block Skills Hub" showcasing Booker as the largest foodservice butcher in the UK, an outdoor street food zone, a Chef's Station with live cooking demonstrations focused on chicken trends, an expanded beverage section (including Venus Wine and Spirit Merchants' portfolio — see below), a Christmas Zone for seasonal lines, and a coffee zone with barista demonstrations. Booker's catering managing director framed the event around helping operators cut costs through the "Value Locked" price-freeze promotions and on-the-day exclusives. No post-event coverage (attendance figures, announcements made on the day) had been published in the trade press as of 28 September 2026.

Beyond the trade show, no other new news specific to Booker Group was found for this period.
