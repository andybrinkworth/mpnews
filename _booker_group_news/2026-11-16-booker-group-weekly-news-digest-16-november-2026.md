Booker

Booker has strengthened its foodservice leadership team with a round of senior appointments, reported by Wholesale Manager on 11 November 2026. The new structure takes effect from January 2027 and brings together Booker's offering and foodservice sales across independent operators, national accounts and new business. Leigh Baker continues as Director of Proposition, shaping Booker's end-to-end customer offer across range, pricing, service, digital journeys and customer experience. Kathy Gerrard joins in a newly created role, Director of CRM and Sales Enablement, leading sales effectiveness and the sales training programme for the Foodservice division. Dave Jones continues as Director of Independent Sales – North, and Jonathon Langford joins Booker as Director of Independent Sales – South; the two lead regional sales teams for independent operators across the UK. Tom Brown is Director of Sales – Key Accounts, heading the team for larger and multi-site customers with more complex requirements. Matthew Atkins joins as Director of Sales – New Business, to build a function focused on new customer partnerships. Nev Irani, Managing Director of Foodservice at Booker, said the new team gives the division "fresh experience and better reach," and that Booker is still recruiting, with roles listed on the Booker group jobs site.

The Grocer's headline on what appears to be the same round of appointments — "Booker poaches Brakes trio in foodservice leadership shake-up" — suggests three of the new joiners came from foodservice wholesaler Brakes, but we were unable to fetch the full Grocer article to confirm this detail independently, so it is flagged here rather than stated as fact.
